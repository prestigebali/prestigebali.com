# **App Name**: Voyage Zen

## Core Features:

- Destination Showcase: Visually showcase travel destinations with high-quality images and videos.
- Curated Tour Packages: Offer a selection of curated tour packages with detailed itineraries and pricing.
- Intelligent Tour Recommendation: AI powered tool to provide personalized tour recommendations based on user preferences and historical data. Generative AI is used to craft compelling text and image compositions from available data.
- Online Booking & Payment: Enable users to book and pay for tours directly through the website.
- User Reviews & Ratings: Allow users to leave reviews and ratings for completed tours.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: Deep ocean blue (#3498DB) for a sense of trust and openness.
- Background color: Very light blue (#ECF0F1), nearly white, to create a clean and calm feel.
- Accent color: Vivid orange (#E67E22) to highlight important CTAs.
- Font: 'Inter', a grotesque-style sans-serif, suitable for both headlines and body text due to its modern and neutral look.
- Use simple, outline-style icons to represent different tour activities and destinations.
- Employ a grid-based layout with plenty of white space for a clean and organized look.
- Incorporate subtle animations and transitions to enhance the user experience, such as fading effects when loading new content.
